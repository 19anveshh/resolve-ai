# Cancellation policy

Customers may cancel an order while its status is **pending** or
**processing**. Cancellation is available for up to 24 hours after the order
was placed, including the exact 24-hour boundary.

Orders that are shipped, delivered, already cancelled, or refunded cannot be
cancelled under this policy.
