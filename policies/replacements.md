# Replacements policy

Replacements are available for **delivered** orders for up to 30 days after
delivery, including the exact 30-day boundary.

A damaged-item replacement requires a support ticket that matches the order
and evidence confirming the damage. A ticket for another order, or a request
without damage evidence, does not qualify.
